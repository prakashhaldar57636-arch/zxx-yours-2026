02 / MY CREATIONS

Poetry Collection

KHAMOSHI

Kuch baatein lab tak aati nahi,
Kuch yaadein dil se jaati nahi.

Khamoshi bhi keh deti hai sab,
Har kahani alfaazon mein aati nahi.

UMEED

Raat gehri ho toh hone do,
Khwaabon ka diya jalne do.

Raaste khud mil jaayenge,
Bas dil ko chalne do.

EHSAAS

Har ehsaas ko naam nahi milta,
Har khwaab ko mukaam nahi milta.

Kuch log dil mein reh jaate hain,
Jinse dobara salaam nahi milta.

ADHOORI KAHANI

Kuch panne abhi khaali hain,
Kuch yaadein abhi sawaali hain.

Kahani khatam nahi hui,
Bas kuch baatein adhoori hain.

UNSPOKEN FEELINGS

Some words are never spoken,
Some hearts are never understood.

Behind every silent smile,
Lives a story untold.

LOST IN MEMORIES

I found you in the silence,
I lost you in the crowd.

Some memories speak softly,
But their echoes are loud.

THE LAST STAR

Even when the sky turns grey,
A little star still finds its way.

Darkness never owns the night,
Every soul deserves its light.

BEYOND WORDS

Not every feeling needs a name,
Not every heart plays the same.

Some emotions live inside,
Where words and silence collide.

Sample verses: replace these with your own original poetry.
